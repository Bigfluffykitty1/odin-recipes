Sheet pan pesto chicken and veggies

Ingredients

  • 2 chiken breasts
  • 1-2 cups of your favorite veggie
  • 2-3 tablespoons basil pesto
  • Olive oil, salt, and pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 425 F. Place the chicken breasts and vegetables on a baking sheet.
  2. Drizzle the vegetables with olive oil and season everything with salt and pepper. Spread the pesto evenly over the top of the chicken breasts.
  3. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are tender.
