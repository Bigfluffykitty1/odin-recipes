Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Beans (One-Skillet)

Ingredients

  • 2 cans of beans (cannellini or chickpeas, rinsed and drained)
  • 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream or chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste

Instructions

  1. In a skillet over medium heat, add the minced garlic and sun-dried tomatoes, cooking for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Add the beans, heavy cream (or stock), and tomato paste.
  3. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, let it thicken for about 5 minutes, and season with salt and pepper before serving.
