5-Minute Bang Bang Salmon (Air Fryer)

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon sweet chili sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon Sriracha (or more to taste)

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, mix the mayonnaise, sweet chili sauce, and Sriracha to create your "bang bang" sauce.
  2. Place the salmon fillets in your air fryer basket and spread the sauce evenly over the top of each fillet.
  3. Air fry at 400 F for about 10 minutes until the salmon is flaky and the sauce is caramelized.
